ℹ️ About Customer CRM

🎯 Overview

Customer CRM is a modern, full-stack Customer Relationship Management application designed to help organizations manage their customer data efficiently with advanced hierarchical relationship tracking.

✨ Key Features

  • Customer Management: Complete CRUD operations for customer records
  • Hierarchical Relationships: Define parent-child relationships with unlimited depth
  • Entity Classification: Categorize as Individual, Entity, or Corporate
  • Data Export: Export to CSV and Excel formats with one click
  • Interactive Grid: Powered by AG Grid with sorting, filtering, and pagination
  • Relationship Visualization: Beautiful modal interface displaying full family trees
  • Real-time Updates: Live data synchronization between frontend and backend
